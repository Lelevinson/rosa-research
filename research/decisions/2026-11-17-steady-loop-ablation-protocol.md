## Question, base, and arm contrasts

The question is which pieces of the *existing* fixed C1 loop matter together. The base is pilot [`harness.py`](../studies/small_model_harness_20261115/harness.py): 12 domain tools plus `final_answer`, errors as model-visible tool observations, strict schema pre-check before Pydantic, `final_answer` plus its prompt and one invalid-answer retry. Its observation-only guard remains active. Round-1 gate, bare-text nudge and list extension, round-2 sweep and answer rewrite, and H2 are all **off** in every arm. Calling all 13 offered tools “12” would omit the finish tool. This is a component test, not a novelty claim; ordinary feedback loops, e.g. [ReAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629), already interleave actions and observations.

## Frozen reading rules and numbers

For each removal compare **fresh concurrent C1 minus removal**, paired by cell. Report 2×2 support transitions, both-supported, C1-only, removal-only, neither; total support /40, per-model /10, aborts, no-answer, cap hits, tool errors, all-episode requests, input/output tokens, native ROS operations and blocked write attempts, time and missing usage. Report costs per planned episode and per supported answer, plus matched-both-supported cost sensitivity; never drop failures for the main cost. Record exposure: actual error observations, pre-check rejections/coercions, use of extra tools, and finish vs text behavior. No exposure or incomplete logs → **NOT SHOWN**, not a pass.

Predeclare **important benefit** only when fresh C1 has ≥3 more supported answers per 40 (+7.5 percentage points) and at least two C1-only disagreements have a trace-supported mechanism chain tied to the component. Three is the nearest conservative integer to half the prior 5/80 threshold, which was chosen to exceed the observed three-cell difference between 80-episode historical C1 runs. These are pragmatic dev-screening thresholds, not significance or confidence intervals; noise and correlated tasks can still explain a gap. **Important harm** uses the symmetric threshold favoring the removal and at least two attributable removal-only traces. Audit all discordances and component exposures blind to the arm aggregate; a single attributable severe wrong answer or blocked write is a safety finding regardless of threshold. If the net gap is smaller, mechanism attribution is absent, or contradictory outcomes dominate: **INCONCLUSIVE / NO CLEAR EFFECT**, not “unnecessary.” Report historical pilot-C1 sensitivity separately without replacing the primary comparison; if it disagrees, disclose the instability rather than selecting the favorable result.

For efficiency, report only after supported-complete outcome and compare all-episode costs; flag a ≥10% change in total requests or total tokens as practically worth reviewing, provided at least two trace-supported exposure cases explain its direction and no important correctness harm appears. Ten percent is a pragmatic screening scale, not a measured noise bound: round 1's 14% request saving was not attributable, so totals alone cannot pass. Include same-cell absolute differences and event-level costs; extra native reads count even if one model request disappears. Do not claim statistical significance, a causal component effect, or held-out generalization from historical controls. Results select what to investigate/freeze, not a proof the components are interchangeable.

## Preflight and provenance

Observed need: C1 70/80 vs A1 47/80, with prompt, menu, error handling and termination all different; identical C1 rerun 67/80 and substantial same-cell nondeterminism. The fairness job isolates one stock-executor contrast, not C1's components. Strong ordinary control: stock executor + deconflicted prompt + errors as text (E1), with all schemas and rights documented; it lacks C1's other features. A removal changes only the stated runtime action, e.g. invalid arguments are rejected by C1 before Pydantic but reach its stock parser without the pre-check. Closest negative: [round 1](../studies/build_round1_20261115/REPORT.md) reported apparent gains without mechanism firing; Stage 3d's prompt change lost correctness. The [finish-gate check](2026-11-17-finish-gate-check.md) recommends leaving C1 unchanged; no new gate is included. The [custodian menu check](2026-11-17-menu-coverage-and-rule.md) describes pool-level coverage and unresolved alternatives without revealing held-out cases. Fairness and these ablations do not establish novelty. No source-specific answer map or scorer oracle goes to the model. The fresh control reduces session confounding; one seed, nondeterminism and unknown actual runtime remain limitations.
