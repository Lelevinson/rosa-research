# Amendment A2 to the thinking-escalation protocol: replication with new seeds and a capped arm (dated 2026-11-19, before any episode of it, smoke included)

Owner's decision of 2026-11-19 (after part A was reported). The frozen [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) (revision R1, SHA-256 `0e9fbdf6dc499a61aa1ec1285a8824d240cfd54de26f8b514697cb7e8a9d49a5`) and [AMENDMENT_A1.md](AMENDMENT_A1.md) (`72be5bc9...`) are unchanged except as stated here. Part A (160 episodes, seeds 1-2) is complete and reported; its numbers, class and rows are not altered. Development evidence only.

## 1. What is added: replication R (phase `replA2`)
- **Tasks (18):** the 10 standard development tasks of part A (stage 3 `u3`, `p3`, `h4`, `v2`; e1h `e1h_amb_1`, `e1h_amb_2`, `e1h_abs_1`, `e1h_abs_2`, `e1h_chg_1`, `e1h_chg_2`) **plus the 8 name-fix tasks** (`nf_single_1`, `nf_single_2`, `nf_multi_1`, `nf_multi_2`, `nf_amb_1`, `nf_amb_2`, `nf_exact`, `nf_absent`), as in the rendering live run. **Models (4):** as before. **New seeds 3 and 4.** **Three arms** shuffled together within each fixture session: `FULL`, `ESCALATE` (unchanged), `ESCALATE_CAPPED` (defined below). One session per (model, environment): 12 sessions (stage3, e1h, namefix). **18 x 4 x 2 x 3 = 432 episodes** (144 per arm). Same settings as part A (temperature 0.7, `num_ctx` 65536, `num_predict` 4096, 40 iterations, HTTP 240 s, episode 1,800 s, round-2 oracle with the 5 s retry). Master seed 20261125, shuffle key includes the phase name. Run through the exam queue runner with the study's own frozen plan; the manifest hash is frozen in `FREEZE.json` (`manifest_replA2`) and checked by the queue before the first request.
- **Arm `ESCALATE_CAPPED`:** identical to `ESCALATE` (same triggers, window of 2, same level per model) **plus a per-episode cap of 6 escalated requests**: once 6 requests of the episode have been sent with thinking on, no further request of that episode is escalated (a trigger no longer opens a window); thinking stays off for the rest of the episode. Triggers are still logged. A `cap_reached` event is logged once.
- **Name-fix tasks** are scored by scorer v2.0 as in the rendering live run; no name-fix rule or renderer is used in any arm; the arms differ only in the thinking setting.
- **Smoke first (not in totals):** `qwen3.5:9b`, tasks `u3` and `nf_single_1`, seed 3, three arms = 6 episodes, checked by `smoke_check_a2.py` (override per request equals the window and cap rule recomputed from the logged triggers; prompts and schemas identical across arms; name-fix scoring present). The cap's behaviour is tested by unit tests (the smoke may not reach 6 escalated requests).

## 2. Reading rules for the replication (frozen now; the protocol's rules of section 5 apply unchanged)
- **R1, the replication:** `ESCALATE` against `FULL` on the replication episodes of all 18 tasks (seeds 3-4, 144 cells per arm) with the unchanged reading rule: mechanism bar M (pooled share of trigger events whose next request makes a progress call at least 10 points higher in `ESCALATE`, higher in at least 2 of the 4 models, counting only models with at least 15 such events in both arms), harm flags (supported lower by more than 3 cells; requests up 15% or more; timeouts + cap endings + no-answer endings up 5 or more; length-cut responses up 10 or more), cost flags, classes A-D. Because there are now 144 cells per arm the same absolute thresholds are used (they are conventions, stated before the run); the report also gives the supported difference as a share.
- **R2, combined:** `ESCALATE` against `FULL` on the **10 shared tasks, seeds 1-4** (part A rows plus the replication rows of those 10 tasks), same rule, same thresholds. Per-model rows for both R1 and R2.
- **Task-clustered intervals:** for the supported difference (points), the ratio of total requests, the ratio of total input + output tokens and the O2 difference, a **cluster bootstrap over tasks** (a task carries its models, seeds and episodes; 5,000 draws, fixed seed): over 18 tasks for R1 and over 10 for R2. Reported next to the point estimates; the class is still decided by the conventions above, not by the intervals.
- **gpt-oss:** its rows and contrasts are reported but **marked "not interpretable"** in every table (part A showed request 1 differing between arms in 16 of 20 cells with identical prompt and settings; its `false` level is medium-like and not recorded). It stays in the pooled counts (the protocol's rule) and a version without it is shown next to the pooled one.
- **Design check, `ESCALATE_CAPPED` against `ESCALATE` (replication episodes only, descriptive, no class and no bar):** (i) **runaway episodes** = episodes with at least 10 requests sent with thinking on, or a length-cut response while thinking was on; (ii) estimated thinking tokens and output tokens in total and per escalated request; (iii) episodes in which the cap was reached; (iv) no-answer endings, cap (iteration) endings, backend errors, length cuts; (v) supported, requests, tokens, wall time. Per model. Whether the cap reduces runaway without losing the progress effect is read from these numbers, not decided by a rule.
- **Part B (`ALWAYS`)**: runs only if the replication ends by **03:00 Asia/Taipei on 2026-11-20** with no stop condition; otherwise it is held for the owner. Its reading remains as in amendment A1 (cross-session, descriptive).
