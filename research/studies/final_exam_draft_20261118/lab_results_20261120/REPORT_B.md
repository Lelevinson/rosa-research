## Open search (part B; unfiltered)
- **My sampler's false positive** (above) shows the contention rule can mark our own processes; it was caught only because the mini exam logged a contended episode on a quiet GPU. The 5 s sampler can also miss a runner that starts and finishes between samples, and rule (c) depends on `others_mib` being exact.
- **A retried cell is a new random draw** (run 1 pair above). If contention were common, retries would change the sampled episodes; the exam report must state the retry count per cell, and a contended episode that happened to be good is dropped by the rule (outcome-independent, but not outcome-neutral for any single cell).
- **`check.py` could not have run in the container as written** (`nvidia-smi` missing); the checklist step 13 verify would have failed on a tooling detail. Fixed; the 1 Hz sampler replaces the in-script polls.
- **granite4.2:8b is 9 % outside VRAM at `num_ctx` 65536** on the 16 GB card and gemma4's residency is unmeasurable from Ollama: if either joins an exam the residency has to be taken from `nvidia-smi` and tokens per second.
- **Memory headroom:** gpt-oss:20b leaves about 2 GB on the card; the desktop daemon holds 249 MiB; the 16 GB card is shared, so a neighbour with a 3 GB job would trigger offload of gpt-oss (rule b/c would mark it, speed would drop).
- **The stack wait of 35 s is a lab-specific timing**; the laptop script uses 60 s. If the Nav2 episode runner is built, its start procedure must be timed on the lab (initial pose before the lifecycle deadline).
