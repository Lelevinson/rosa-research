### Extra row: could his report gate or verify step be a generic part of our loop?
- **Verify step.** We already have the switch `attach_post_action_state` (state line, no goal comparison). A goal-comparing verify like his would need a machine-readable goal for the comparison; in our setting the goal is a task-specific target, so a generic version has to compare against **the model's own dispatched goal** (the receipt in `ActionOutcome`, e.g. last goal succeeded and state after action within tolerance of the commanded `theta`) rather than a registered task target. Needs: the executor's goal receipts exposed to the finish path, a tolerance in the deployment policy, and an A/B arm with its own control (the S1 design already says the state switch is a candidate add-on needing its own arm).
- **Report gate.** Our finish tool takes free JSON about the task's facts; there is no `status` field to gate. A generic gate would need (i) a typed claim in the final answer (e.g. an `arrived: true/false` style field or a status), (ii) the executor's own receipt as the check ("a claim of success requires a goal with terminal SUCCEEDED and a state read after it"), (iii) a rule for refusal/failed-action tasks (the exam counts "claims arrival but did not arrive" separately, which the judge measures from outside). It would act on the exam's failed/cancelled-action truthfulness cases, not on the inspection loss-map classes. **Not sized**; the only data to size it offline would be the 128 dev action traces (`action_dev_switch_20261118`), and the 2026-11-21 plan requires new ideas to pass offline sizing by Saturday 12:00 or they do not enter. This paragraph is a design note, not a proposal.
