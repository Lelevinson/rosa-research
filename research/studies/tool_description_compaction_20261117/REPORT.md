## 3. Reading the traces (hand reading of every discordant cell and every cell with E2 events)
**Where the extra requests and errors come from (measured):** two bonsai cells produce 51 of the 56 extra requests. (1) `v2`: FULL 5 requests, COMPACT 40 requests (the iteration limit; no answer in either arm; both unsupported): COMPACT looped on guessed names (`service_info` of bare `cedar_reset` returning `{'error': ''}` 19 times and variants of the service name). (2) `e1h_chg_2`: FULL 11 requests, supported; COMPACT 27 requests, unsupported: after a failed `topic_echo` it went into a detour of 15 `read_log` calls, 8 of them rejected by the pre-check (`num_lines` or `level_filter` passed as a string, expected integer or enum), then answered `final_mode: idle`. Without these two cells the arms are close: requests 202 (FULL) vs 207 (COMPACT), supported 31 vs 33 (descriptive, not a replacement for the frozen comparison).
**Is the text change the cause? (interpretation)** For the `read_log` detour, the rule changed little: the description of `read_log` went from 420 to 391 characters and keeps the tool line and all four argument lines; the typing errors (string for integer) are model errors that the schema type already states in `parameters`, which the rule does not touch. The two trajectories already differ at request 2 (COMPACT passed `return_echoes: True` to `topic_echo`, FULL did not), which is a sampling choice. For `v2` bonsai, the guessing loop is the known failure of this task (see the ablation), also with the original descriptions (lfm3 and bonsai failed it in the ablation's FULL arm). I found no description-related chain among the extra errors, but the one-seed design cannot exclude that shorter text makes such loops slightly more likely.
**Discordant cells (5; all differ from request 1 or 2, directions mixed):** FULL-only: bonsai `e1h_abs_1` (FULL called `node_info` on the exact name, COMPACT searched with a `node_list` pattern and then answered for the look-alike node `/e1h/ember/mistral`), bonsai `e1h_chg_2` (above). COMPACT-only: gpt-oss `e1h_abs_1` (the reverse of the bonsai case: FULL went to the look-alike, COMPACT answered the exact name), lfm3 `e1h_chg_2` (FULL read `param_list` first and answered the stale `idle`; COMPACT read the three parameters in one request and got `run`), gpt-oss `h4` (FULL's answer lacked the required fields after a call with an empty topic, COMPACT's was complete). None has a trace-supported chain from the description text.

**Note on TABLES.md:** its "cap hits" count is 0 because the executor returns the max-iterations text as the answer without an error; counted from the traces, 1 episode ended at the 40-request limit (COMPACT `v2` bonsai, above) and none in FULL.

## 4. Interpretation and decision input
- The rule is safe in the sense of this test (no drop, no changed parameters, regex/glob errors unchanged at 2 and 2) but **does not pay in the frozen sense**: the mechanical saving is small (about 117 tokens per request, 3%) and was swamped by trajectory-length differences in this run; the bar (b) of -3% on whole-arm input per request cannot be passed or failed meaningfully at this noise level. A fair size of the saving would be the exact per-request figure above times the requests: about 32k tokens on the 274 COMPACT requests (2.6% of its input).
- For the Saturday freeze: per the protocol the answer is "not eligible"; as a separate, descriptive point, the rule would reduce the fixed per-request input by about 117 tokens (about 6% of the 2,000-token first request, 3% of the mean) if nothing else changed.
- Larger savings would need a different lever (the schema size measurement showed 41% of the schema characters are descriptions, but the rule keeps argument lines; dropping `final_answer` costs 87 to 125 tokens and 7 cells in the ablation).

## 5. Validity, infrastructure, limits
One oracle first-try failure (qwen3.5 e1h `before`: the daemon listed 0 of 14 fixture nodes about a second after its restart; the 5-second retry listed 14 of 14; no exclusion). It was the first read after the daemon restart of amendment 4, and the only first-try oracle failure in the 34 full-run sessions run since amendment 4 (round 2 resume 6, fairness 8, ablation 8, name-fix 4, this test 8); it did not affect any episode (it happens before the first episode of the session). All other valid episodes are in the denominators. Limits: one seed, 40 cells, noise about 4 cells and large episode-length noise; two bonsai cells dominate every token and request total; development fixtures only; the rule is one automatic rule (first sentence plus one line per documented argument) and says nothing about other rules or other tool sets. Not a novelty or significance claim.
