## 1. Candidate 1: ledger name resolution at input
**Measured (P1; P2 beside it).**
- **Fires** (not-found call, no leading slash, exactly one L-res candidate): P1 82 in 37 episodes (lfm3 34, qwen3.5 10, gpt-oss 5, bonsai 33; tools: node_info 53, param_list 15, param_get 14); by task nf_multi_1 41, h4 15, u3 13, e1h_abs_1 3, nf_single_2 3, v2 3, nf_single_1 2, e1h_amb_1 2. P2 486 fires in 237 episodes (bonsai 268, lfm3 139, qwen3.5 46, gpt-oss 33). The task-text ledger variant gives the same P1 fires (82; 2 more episodes). 65 of 82 P1 fires bind to a **joined name** (never one string in a result; LEDGER_DEFINITION.md); 4 are slash-only (typed equals the name without its leading slash).
- **Truth of each binding vs fixture truth (P1):** needed 67, **wrong 0**, not needed 13, **absent-made-present 2**. P2: needed 305, wrong 0, not needed 150, **absent-made-present 31**. `differ` (the model later used another entity for the typed string): 0 in P1, 0 in P2 (L-res).
- **The harm case (hand-read):** `block_0011/015-e1h_abs_1-FULL-bonsai-2_4b-s6` (`research/_artifacts/exam/lossmap/runs_all/.../trace.jsonl`): the task asks whether `/ember/mistral` runs; `ros2_node_list` returned `/e1h/ember/mistral`, a different node (a look-alike in another namespace); the model then typed `ember/mistral` (no slash) twice, got not-found, and correctly answered `running: false`. Under rule IN the typed string `ember/mistral` has exactly one L-res candidate, `/e1h/ember/mistral`, so both calls would have been executed on the look-alike and returned a real node. A third fire in the same episode (`e1h/ember/mistral`) binds to the same look-alike; the frozen rule classes it "not needed" because it is not a suffix of the absent name, so by the frozen count the harm is 2 fires, by a plain reading 3 of the 3 fires in absent-entity tasks. P2 has 31 such fires in **8 episodes** (e.g. `research/_artifacts/runs/small_model_harness_20261115/pilot_part2/bonsai-2_4b-e1h/001-e1h_abs_1-A1-bonsai-2_4b-s2/trace.jsonl`); 30 are the `/ember/mistral` look-alike pair (typed `ember/mistral` 27, `mistral` 3) and 1 is `alder/pewter` to `/e1h/alder/pewter` in e1h_abs_2, so the 31 fires are 8 episodes on two fixture pairs, not 31 independent situations.
- **Saved-max requests (P1):** 75 (4.53% of 1,655; lfm3 29, bonsai 34, qwen3.5 8, gpt-oss 4); median k - r = 2. 32 of 82 fires have no later successful call on the bound name (`no_later_use`: no saving counted). **Coverage of the loss map:** 56 of the 147 `name_guess` requests are fire requests (they would become productive, not saved); 35 of the 78 `name_recovery` requests lie in a saved interval; saved requests typed guess or recovery: 47 = 20.9% of the 218. P2 saved-max 499 (3.96%).
- Fires inside the 45 unsupported P1 episodes: 24 fires in 8 episodes (22 in wrong-conclusion episodes, 2 in cap endings).
- **OUT replay (name-rewrite v2 unchanged on final answers, scorer v2.0):** P1 279 episodes with an answer object (9 without; 24 of them free-text answers, which the live rule could only act on through the finish tool), 11 rewritten (22 rewrites); supported 243 to 252; **supported to unsupported 0**, unsupported to supported 9; of the 26 wrong-conclusion episodes **9 flip to supported** (lfm3 nf_single_1; qwen3.5 nf_multi_2; bonsai u3 x2, nf_single_1 x3, nf_multi_1 x2) and 2 are rewritten but stay unsupported (bonsai nf_multi_1 s5, s8). P2: 22 rewritten episodes, 0 down, 19 up.
**Bars.** H1 **missed** (P1 2, P2 31 absent-made-present; wrong 0). S1 met (75 >= 50; second branch: 47 of 218 = 20.9% < 25%, see deviations). S2 met (4 models; largest task share 50%). H2 met. **Class FAIL-HARM.**
**Interpretation (hypotheses).** The failure is not a wrong binding to a needed entity (0 of 82) but that a ledger-unique suffix is not a name the task's world agrees with: the task gives an absolute name that does not exist, a similar real node exists elsewhere, and the rule cannot know the typed string was meant literally. Whether a rule that also checks the task text (do not rebind a string that is a suffix of a task-text name) would remove the P1 and P2 harm was not measured and would be a new rule needing its own freeze. The 13 "not needed" P1 bindings include role mismatches (a service name typed as a node bound to the same service name, bonsai v2, `block_0010/006-v2-FULL-bonsai-2_4b-s5`), which re-run the failing call under a "Resolved" message.

## 3. Candidate 3: list results carry values
**Measured (P1; P2 beside it).** Successful `ros2_param_list` calls: **111** (lfm3 24, qwen3.5 37, gpt-oss 1, bonsai 49), **107 followed by a successful `param_get` on the same node** (qwen3.5 37 of 37, bonsai 48 of 49, lfm3 21 of 24, gpt-oss 1 of 1; matches the loss map). In all 107 the first parameter read afterwards was **already named in the task text or an earlier result before the list** (the list was avoidable by a direct get). Inline size: median 105 characters with values (original 99 or so); **0 of 111 lists above the 2,000-character cap** (P2: 3 of 698), so the cap never binds in P1 (it is untested for large nodes). Added characters per eligible list: mean 20. 64% of the inline values are **estimates** (parameters never read in the episode, mostly `start_type_description_service` and `use_sim_time`; median value length of the population, 5 characters).
**Saved-max requests: 95 (5.74% of 1,655)**: lfm3 22, qwen3.5 28, gpt-oss 1, bonsai 44; tasks e1h_amb_1 25, nf_amb_1 22, h4 17, nf_multi_1 15, nf_amb_2 5, e1h_chg_2 3, and 5 more tasks with 1-2. P2 558 (4.42%). **Stale-value risk:** 3 of the saved gets are in `e1h_chg_2` (P1 examples: `block_0002/012-e1h_chg_2-FULL-lfm3_1.7b-s7` request 2, `block_0011/003-...-bonsai-2_4b-s8` request 14, `block_0011/018-...-bonsai-2_4b-s6` request 11); P2 41.
**Tokens (H1).** Added input tokens over the episodes (values riding in all later requests): **1,873** estimated (lfm3 488, qwen3.5 529, gpt-oss 5, bonsai 851) against **334,440** measured input tokens of the saved requests (lfm3 87,160, qwen3.5 92,359, gpt-oss 2,140, bonsai 152,781); added exceeds saved in 5 of the 57 episodes with an eligible list. P2: 25,406 vs 2,745,226, 31 of 340 episodes.
**Bars.** S1 met (5.74% >= 2%), S2 met (4 models; gpt-oss has 1 saved get, so effectively 3), H1 met in P1 and P2. **Class PASS on the frozen bars.**
**Interpretation (hypotheses).** (i) Saved-max assumes the model uses the inline values and sends no further `param_get`; the model's current habit (list before every get although the name is known) shows it does not need the list to find names, so whether it would also ignore inline values is not measured here. (ii) The saved tokens are the full input tokens of each saved request (generous), the added tokens are small because P1 parameter lists are short (3-4 parameters); a node with many parameters would approach the 2,000-character cap, a case the traces do not contain. (iii) The closest prior mechanism in this repository is the list sweep (whole-episode token use -1.4%, native operations +7.8%, benefit not demonstrated); this study does not show that C3 differs from it in effect, and no control arm exists.

No k has 0 harm in P1 or in P2 (smallest k with 0 harm: none; the table k = 6 is used only as a label). The 5 capped P1 episodes all trigger: at k = 6 at requests 7 (lfm3 nf_exact s7), 12, 8, 11 (bonsai v2 s5, s7, s8) and 24 (bonsai nf_single_2 s5) of 40; at k = 4 at requests 5, 10, 6, 9, 17.
**The P1 harm case (hand-read):** `block_0011/003-e1h_chg_2-FULL-bonsai-2_4b-s8` ended supported after 16 requests: requests 2-12 were failed or repeated `topic_echo`/`topic_info` calls on `/flow` (no success result, so non-novel), the needed `param_get` reads came at requests 14-15, the trigger at k = 6 fires after request 9. A forced answer there would have been written without the facts. Harm cases are therefore runs that **fail for a stretch and then recover**, the opposite of a loop, and the guard cannot tell them apart by whether results are new, because failed calls count as no progress by the frozen definition.
**Bars.** H1 **missed at every k** (P1 and P2), so **FAIL-HARM**; S1 would be met at every k (>= 50) but is not applicable after a harm failure.
**Interpretation (hypotheses).** The guard's size is real in P1 (170-212 requests, mostly bonsai v2 and the 5 capped episodes) but the frozen novelty rule treats failed calls as no progress; a rule that counted failed calls differently would be a new rule and a new freeze, and its harm would have to be measured again. Where all support facts were present at the trigger (6 of 12 unsupported episodes at k = 6) a forced answer *could* be right, but offline there is no evidence it would be.

## 7. Deviations from the protocol and implementation notes (no bar or rule was changed after counts)
1. **Statistics added after the first look at the counts:** scan field `all_calls_ok` and the C2 post-hoc splits (section 2), and a corrected "estimated parameter share" in C3 (the first aggregate divided by the number of lists, giving 212%; now by the number of parameters, 64%). The counts were produced twice (the second run after adding these fields); the bars and first-run counts are unchanged.
2. **C1 S1 second branch is ambiguous in the source file:** read literally (saved-max of all request types >= 25% of 218 = 55) it is stricter than the first branch (50) and never decides; read as "saved requests typed guess or recovery" it is 47 of 218 = 20.9% (not met). The class is decided by the first branch (75 >= 50) and the harm bar, so the reading does not change the class.
3. **C2 derived-name guesses:** the frozen definition (service and topic names from successful list or info results) is the primary count; a variant that also takes task-text role words ("Service /...", "Topic /...") is reported beside it, as announced here, not in PROTOCOL.md.
4. **Absent-made-present** is applied as frozen (typed is a trailing-segment suffix of the absent name); a third fire in the same P1 episode that binds to the same look-alike was classed "not needed" by the rule (section 1).
5. **Capped episode** (C4, any population): no answer object and (>= 40 requests or "iteration" in the end text or aborts). P1 gives the loss map's 5. P2 has 39 by this rule (its runners may have had other caps; not checked).
6. **B0 data check:** P1 re-scored support equals the logged support in 288 of 288; P2 has 25 disagreements (logged vs re-scored; kept, as in `candidate_counts_20261118`, where they were excluded only from its (c) and (d)).
7. **Git:** `.git/info/exclude` has a `/research/` rule, so the new folder needs `git add -f` (existing studies are tracked the same way); no ignore rule was edited.
8. The `.pyc` file shown modified in the working tree at the start of the session (`action_dev_switch_20261118/__pycache__/run_action_study.cpython-312.pyc`) predates this work and was not touched; the runs set `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`.

## 8. Limits
Development tasks, 18 in P1, four small models, one fixture family per group; P2 is not independent of P1. All sizes are upper bounds under favourable readings (a saved request is removed with no cost elsewhere; the model follows the new output). No model, ROS node or control arm was run, so behaviour change (a model that ignores inline values, a model that still retries a typed name, a forced answer that is wrong) is unmeasured. Hand checks: the absent-entity C1 case, the C4 harm case, one C2 scan sample and three C3 saved-get samples were read in the traces; the C1 `needed` class and the C2 scan detector were not independently re-derived. Teammate branches (`rosa-contexts` `member/*`) were not read for this offline task. No novelty claim is made for any candidate; whether C1, C3 or C4 equal earlier mechanisms is not established here.

## Open search (other patterns noticed; counts and trace paths; unfiltered)
1. **Whole-system `ros2_param_list` fails in the fixtures:** 5 of 5 calls without `node_name` returned `{"error": ""}` (e.g. `block_0003/024-nf_exact-FULL-lfm3_1.7b-s8`, `block_0003/026-nf_amb_1-FULL-lfm3_1.7b-s8`); the models then fall back to per-node calls. Not examined whether the fixture or the tool is at fault.
2. **Look-alike entities across namespaces** (`/e1h/ember/mistral` vs absent `/ember/mistral`): two pairs account for all absent-made-present fires (P1 1 episode; P2 8 episodes, 31 fires); the loss map's 7 "absent reported present" episodes were not cross-checked against it.
3. **Role-blind ledger:** a service name typed as a node (`plant32/cedar_reset`) has exactly one ledger match, the service name itself, so rule IN would re-run `node_info` on a service name (P1 3 fires, `block_0010/006-v2-FULL-bonsai-2_4b-s5`).
4. **Derived-name guessing is task v2 almost alone** (P1 7 of 7, P2 183 of 185) and mostly lfm3 (135 of 185 in P2); in lfm3 v2 s6 the final answer is the derived name `/plant32/cedar` (`block_0001/009-v2-FULL-lfm3_1.7b-s6`).
5. **Value-filter tasks** (`*_amb_*`) generate scans and avoidable list/get pairs (C2 10 of 19 scans; C3 e1h_amb_1 25 and nf_amb_1 22 of 95 saved gets); both candidates count requests in these tasks; the C2&C3 request overlap is 0 (not examined why).
6. **Recovery after a long failure stretch** (the C4 harm pattern) also appears in P2 supported episodes: 11 harmed at k = 6 (v2 7, e1h_amb_1 2, h4 1, e1h_chg_1 1); `.../pilot_part2/...` paths are in `results.json` (`c4.6.harm_cases`).
7. **All C4 triggers in P1 are lfm3 or bonsai** (qwen3.5 and gpt-oss never trigger at k = 4-6 in P1; P2 has 7 and 0 at k = 6).
8. **Answer-side rewrite already flips 9 of 26 wrong conclusions in P1** (section 1) without any input-side change; the two rewritten-but-unsupported cases (bonsai nf_multi_1 s5, s8) were not examined further.
9. **P2 logged-vs-re-scored support disagrees in 25 episodes** (`results.json`, `logged_vs_rescored_disagreements`; ids in `disagreement_run_ids` for P2, first 10).
