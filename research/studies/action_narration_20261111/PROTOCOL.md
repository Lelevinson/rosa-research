Stage1 positive signal requires at least two mutually fully supported tasks,
no loss in strict supported completion versus A, aggregate total generated tokens,
decode and episode wall lower on mutually supported pairs, and savings in both
applications rather than one incomplete outlier. This is exploratory, not a
statistical noninferiority test. Review a disconfirming case before any win claim.
If controls tie, quality drops or no decode saving, stop this intervention. Even
if B/C help, absent D mechanism means systems-only finding, not new algorithm.
No prospective held-out study follows automatically from an engineering result.
