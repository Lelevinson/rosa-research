# Dead-end relation lookup: offline replay protocol (frozen before any number is computed)

Dated 2026-11-18, Asia/Taipei. Source: the owner's queued request of 2026-11-18 (CPU only, do not disturb the running GPU job, no live run until the owner decides). Offline, on saved traces; development evidence only (synthetic fixtures, four small models, authored dev tasks); not a held-out result, not a novelty claim. Scorer v2.0 untouched. No held-out custodian file is opened; no task or fixture file of the held-out pools is used. Every number is a prediction from recorded traces. What a model would do after the lines appeared is unknown.

## 0. Preflight record (AGENTS.md; ROSA_RESEARCH_CONTEXT 12.2 / 14.2)
1. **Observed opportunity.** Recorded not-found lookups: 334 `ros2_node_info` "Unable to find node", 69 `ros2_param_get` and 51 `ros2_param_list` "Node not found", 59 `ros2_service_info` `{"error": ""}` (first 700 of 1,832 saved episodes, shape check only; the counts of this study are computed by the frozen script). `candidate_counts_20261118` counted slashless not-found fires (a) on 17 (task, model) pairs; `write_tail_repeat_20261118` found repeat loops; the dead-end episodes' request cost is what this study measures. Honest denominators: the 1,832 episodes are not independent samples (tasks, seeds and fixtures recur across arms).
2. **Candidate vs control.** Candidate: at a dead-end result the system searches the episode environment's graph for the name's path segments and appends at most 10 factual lines, no ranking, no "did you mean". Control (strongest ordinary mechanism): the model itself calls `ros2_node_list`/`ros2_topic_list`/`ros2_service_list` with the stock `pattern` argument (a regular expression, matched as `.*pattern.*`, in `src/rosa/tools/ros2.py`), or lists everything. The difference: the system does the search automatically, across node, topic, service and parameter names and the publisher/subscriber/service relations, in the same result, without a model request. The information is available to the control with the same tools; the candidate saves requests, it adds no new capability. The graph read is a framework action (cost: native ROS operations, not counted here; stated).
3. **Closest prior mechanisms.** Offline replay 2 job 1 (`offline_replay2_20261116`): trigger = a successful per-entity read whose result names no other entity, action = run other per-entity tools; 0 requests saved for the name lookup as written. Stall hint v2 (`stall_hint_v2_20261118`): hint built only from names the episode had already seen; failed its offline bar; its stated limit (the needed entity is often unseen) is the gap this candidate tries to fill. Canonical rendering / name fix: they rewrite what the model has seen. This candidate adds names the episode has not seen. Not a novelty claim: related to "did-you-mean" suggestion, entity linking and graph-augmented observations; no literature review done here (unverified).
4. **Evaluation and cost.** Offline replay over all saved episodes with frozen bars; coverage and an upper bound on savings; harm exposure (look-alikes); tokens added. The upper bound assumes the model acts on the lines at once; it is not an expected saving.
5. **Next action and stop rule.** One offline run with this protocol. If the bars of section 5 are not all met, the idea stops (no live run, no variant this week). If they are met the result only makes it eligible for the owner's decision on a live test (not authorised here). Distinct from old work: this is not a repeat of job 1 (different trigger and action) nor of stall hint v2 (graph names, not seen names).
**Status of the idea: not yet justified as a method component.** Predicted risk, untested (H): the `e1h_abs_*` tasks ask about an absent node whose name is a suffix of a real node (`e1h_abs_2`: `/alder/pewter` vs `/e1h/alder/pewter`), so a lookup is exactly what could mislead.

The 1,832 episodes of `candidate_counts_20261118` (`count_candidates.load_population()` and `parse_episode`, unchanged, scorer v2.0 re-score as there), all arms and studies. Reported per model, per task, per arm group (menu arms; stock-executor arms S0, A1, E0, E1) and for the FULL-type subset (`count_candidates.FULL_TYPE`, the unconfounded 12-tool loop). **Separately reported strata:** `V2` (stage-3 task `v2`), `ABS` (`e1h_abs_1`, `e1h_abs_2`), `OTHER` (all remaining tasks).
**Environment graph** per episode: the fixture JSON of its environment: `headroom_diagnostic_20261112/stage3_graph.json` (stage 3), `small_model_harness_20261115/e1h_graph.json` (e1h), `name_fix_applied_20261117/namefix_graph.json` (name-fix). Items: nodes (`fqn`), topics (`pubs`, `subs` names), services (`services`), parameters (`params` keys). Relations: topic published by node, topic subscribed by node, service provided by node, parameter of node. Scripted changes alter parameter values only, never names. Environment of an episode = the `env` of its row.

## 2. Dead end, lines, caps (frozen)
- **Per-entity tools and kind:** `ros2_node_info`, `ros2_param_list`, `ros2_param_get` (kind node), `ros2_topic_info` (topic), `ros2_service_info` (service); write tools and `ros2_topic_echo` excluded. The entity is the parsed call's `entity` (a list argument is one call per item), normalised by one leading slash.
- **Dead end:** such a call that is not a harness message and whose recorded result for that entity (a) matches `episodes.NOT_FOUND` ("not found", "Unable to find", "does not exist", "no such"), or (b) is an `error` entry with an empty message, or (c) is empty (empty or whitespace text, `[]`, `{}`, `null`, `None`). Classes (a), (b), (c) are reported separately. **Fire** = the first dead end per (episode, normalised entity); later dead ends of the same entity are counted but do not fire again.
- **Segments:** the entity split on `/`, empty parts dropped, parts equal to the environment's root namespace segment (`plant32`, `e1h`, `nf`) dropped (they match everything).
- **Search (no ranking, no fuzzy matching):** an item matches a segment if one of its path segments (split on `/`) equals the segment exactly (case-sensitive); for a parameter, if its key equals the segment. Items are scanned in fixture-file order; segments in the order of the entity name.
- **Lines** (one per matching item and relation, duplicates removed; the segment shown is the matched one): `<segment> appears in: node <fqn>`; `<segment> appears in: topic <name> (published by node <fqn>)` and `(subscribed by node <fqn>)`, one line per relation; `<segment> appears in: service <name> (provided by node <fqn>)`; `<segment> appears in: parameter <key> (of node <fqn>)`. A topic or service with no relation yields one line without parentheses. If the dead-end entity is itself an exact item of the same kind in the graph (the lookup failed for another reason), the fire is classed `graph_has_it` and no lines are made (reported; the candidate would add nothing).
- **Caps:** at most 10 lines, and at most 600 characters in total (a fixed budget of about 150 tokens at 4 characters per token, an estimate); a line that would pass either cap is not added and no later line is added; `truncated` is reported. Order is fixture-file order, so the cut is arbitrary, not by relevance (stated limit).
- **Tokens added** per fire = characters / 4 of the lines. Because the lines stay in the history, the cost counted per fire is that figure times the number of requests after the fire's request.

## 3. Measures (frozen)
1. **Fires** per model, task, stratum, arm group; episodes with at least one fire; dead ends and fires by class; `graph_has_it` and empty-lines fires.
2. **Coverage.** Needed entities of the episode's task: support items of type `text` whose string starts with `/`, and the node name of every `param` support item (`absent` items are not needed entities). A fire is **covered** if its lines contain at least one needed entity (as a full name); **covered-new** if that entity had not appeared in any earlier result of the episode. Computed for fires in tasks that have at least one needed entity (all except `ABS`). Reported per model, task, stratum and FULL-type.
3. **Requests after the first dead end** (per episode with a fire: total requests minus the request index of the first fire), sum and median, per model and stratum.
4. **Upper bound on requests the lines could have made unnecessary (labelled upper bound; the model might ignore the lines).** For an episode whose first covered fire is at request `f`: let `g` be the first request after `f` whose successful result text contains a covered needed entity (the point where the episode actually obtained the information the lines gave). Saved-max = `g - f - 1` (at least 0). If no later result contains it: saved-max = (total requests - f - 1), reported as "never obtained" separately. Tokens saved-max = recorded input + output tokens of those requests. The covered entity is chosen as the one with the earliest `g`.
5. **Look-alike exposure (harm).** A fire is a look-alike fire if the dead-end entity is of kind node, is not any node `fqn` in the graph, and the lines contain a node line (`appears in: node`) whose fqn is different from the entity and has the same final path segment, or whose fqn ends with the entity's non-root path (a suffix match). Counted per stratum; for `ABS` every look-alike fire is listed with its task, model, arm, seed and the lines; also for `ABS` the number of episodes whose recorded answer was supported (the correct answer is "absent"), because those are the episodes the lines could turn into a wrong "running".
6. **Tokens added** (section 2) summed, and compared with the tokens saved-max.
Report `V2`, `ABS` and `OTHER` with the same tables. Whole-population totals are given but are not the reading basis because `ABS` fires are harm, not opportunity.

## 5. Bars (frozen; idea "eligible for the owner's decision on a live test" only if all are met)
- **B1 opportunity:** in the FULL-type subset, episodes with a fire (classes a-c, excluding `graph_has_it`) are at least 10% of episodes, and at least 3 models each have 5 or more such episodes.
- **B2 coverage:** among fires in tasks with a needed entity, at least 50% covered and at least 20 covered fires over at least 2 models; the covered-new share is reported and must be at least 25% of all those fires (the lines must give something the episode did not have).
- **B3 upper-bound saving:** saved-max (requests) is at least 3% of all requests of the FULL-type subset and tokens added are smaller than tokens saved-max (the bar of offline replay 2 job 1, kept for comparability; an upper bound, so passing is necessary, not sufficient).
- **B4 harm:** zero look-alike fires in `ABS`. Any `ABS` look-alike fire is a harm flag: the idea is "not eligible as written" whatever B1-B3 show, and the cases are listed.
- **Result classes:** A = B1-B4 met; C = B4 not met (reported, not adopted); B = B4 met and at least one of B1-B3 not met. A result carried by one task (largest task share above 60% of the covered fires or of the saving) is flagged as not general.
- Statements are limited to these fixtures, models and tasks. "Not met" means not seen in recorded traces; it is not proof about a live run.
