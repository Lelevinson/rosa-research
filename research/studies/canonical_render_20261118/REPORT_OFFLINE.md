## Interpretation (labelled)
- The rendering is lossless and score-neutral on every recorded result, and costs about +0.1% of input tokens overall (a few results grow by up to ~1.9k characters, most do not change). Whether a model reads `Node full name: /plant32/anvil` better than the two fields, and whether that removes slashless not-found calls, is untested here; the earlier tool-specific version (pilot `C1+FN`, 63/80 vs C1 65/80) gave no support gain, and its name-error counts were not analysed.
- Generality is **not demonstrated by this corpus**: all derived lines came from one tool's output, because that is the only saved output with a namespace field and a name field. The generic parser reads 14.5% of leaves and leaves the list-shaped results alone. The Nav2 / TurtleBot3 generality check (step 4) is where that would be tested.
- The mechanism's precondition holds for 57% of fires, concentrated in gpt-oss and qwen3.5 (all or nearly all of their fires) and weaker in bonsai (33%). Because 0 of 76 held-out tasks need short-name resolution (custodian aggregate), the value for the held-out set depends on whether models drop the slash in other tasks; this study cannot say.
