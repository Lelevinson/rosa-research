# AutoTool-family continuation: bounded development and negative result

2026-11-12, Asia/Taipei. Research report for the existing ROSA line and the
December2 presentation. **Result: systems integration only; no incremental
algorithmic advantage demonstrated. Live auto-execution remains BLOCKED / NOT
YET JUSTIFIED.** This conclusion follows executable offline development, two
adversarial rule revisions, original-trace replay and independent source review.
It does not follow merely from finding related work or repeating a feasibility memo.

## Question and historical responsibility

Suppose ROSA has just discovered a node. A historical trajectory often inspects
that node next. Can the host safely make that inspection without asking the LLM
again? A successful discovery makes the next action plausible. It does not say
whether the user wanted names only, whether the inspected identity is the right
one, or whether another read would complete the requested procedure. This study
investigates that distinction inside AutoTool-family history continuation.

The central comparison is a selective history-based rule against the strongest
ordinary conditional transition table and unique-origin binder, plus an ordinary
parameterized helper capable of traversing the same authorized path. Correctness
and complete source-supported answers precede call counts. Neither an exact next
action nor a successful native receipt establishes that an action was needed.

The canonical Sections0–19, especially12.2 and14.2, were read before development.
The current request authorizes finite offline work and conditional local read-only
experiments. Old no-run recommendations remain dated history; they are not erased
or treated as the current task. Conversely, old GPT approval does not authorize
new OpenAI inference. No OpenAI or paid-provider inference, model access probe,
ROS fixture setup or native operation was performed here.

Continuity included R19–28 reports, selected earlier raw records and every copied
R27/28 episode, the November7 diagnostic, CodeAct manual review, Missions A/B/D,
CPAW, native-read, action-narration and GPT coverage evidence. The completed MissionC
report and named November6 focused pilot were not found in the searched locations;
their missing detailed reasoning cannot be independently certified. The original64
structured-dependency traces remain missing. The reconstructed archive was never
substituted for them. Exact available sources and these inference limits appear
in [HISTORY_AND_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md](HISTORY_AND_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md).

Historical failures have direct consequences. R27's reported6/14 graph-arm coverage

## Parent mechanisms and independent challenge

The author implementation of [AutoTool](https://github.com/jiajingyyyyyy/AutoTool)
was inspected at commit c7010ff49750ed338fec61556f7797b104a6826e alongside its
[author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.14650v1). TIG stores transitions and
parameter associations. CIPS combines transition evidence with contextual
similarity, then uses thresholds and fallback. Source inspection additionally
confirmed recognized-error suppression and inertia caps. Parameter filling uses
historical equality/membership associations and domain/environment adapters.
A confidence threshold, outcome rejection, parameter flow or LLM fallback is
therefore already parent functionality.

The final publisher PDF could not be inspected; author preprint/final identity
is unverified. Source/paper differences include alpha weighting and selection
from list sources. Full AgentBoard, SimCSE and semantic domain fillers were not
executed. Importing the parent's task-specific semantic adapters would violate
the no-handmapping boundary. Our comparison is explicitly a constrained selector
and common conservative binder, never a faithful reproduction or a claim to beat
the published parent. Unavailable embedding/preprocessing cost remains unknown.

Independent primary-source review also examined methods/ablations in
[PASTE](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.18897v3),
[AWM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/wang25bx.html),
[SpecAgent](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.21463v1), LLMCompiler, ReWOO, CodeAct and
selective-classification work, with mechanism-synonym checks including Tracer,
AuthGraph and AuthorityFlow. PASTE already uses outcome-conditioned patterns and
current-payload argument mappings while overlapping authoritative generation.
AWM includes callable parameterized workflows; ordinary helper competition cannot
be dismissed. SpecAgent has draft/review/commit costs and consequential final
submission. A provenance-security verifier brings semantic inputs and compute;
it is not a free typed binder. Full inspection scopes, source versions and exact
comparisons are in [PRIMARY_SOURCES.md](PRIMARY_SOURCES.md).

Two independent hypotheses remained worth finite development. H1 conditions
admission on public outcome and STOP competition. H2 preserves source identities
for historically associated argument paths. Their possible value is a better
complete-action risk/coverage frontier, not simply adding a feature. Both must
survive an ordinary table and binder receiving the identical features and rights.

## Implemented rules and substantive revisions

The file-only prototype learns grouped tool successors, including normal STOP,
from earlier episodes. It never flattens a multicall model response into several
independent selection turns. A state contains preceding grouped names, explicit
errors, registry schema and receipt shapes. Raw request text is available but the
implemented count estimator does not interpret its semantics. That limitation is
material: names-only and details requests can remain indistinguishable to the
chosen features even though a model could interpret their different text.

For H2, each historical target argument stores every equal and exactly typed
path in the preceding receipt group. The final binder checks one origin in both
training and the current state, the source tool and its arguments, shape and
type. Values are instantiated from current observations. Historic literals are
never blindly reused. Text-only outputs, multiple source candidates, unmatched
receipts and changed identities/schema are rejected. These checks are ordinary
structural evidence discipline; equality is not source authorization.

Iteration1 implemented constrained parent scoring, k1/k2 ngrams and the initial
success/association rule. Eight deterministic synthetic cases covered changed
goal/STOP, error repair, equal-value sources, text-only observations, changed
source identity, changed schema and the already-available list helper. In the
executable learner-based version, the initial rule made four false admissions.
These failures are preserved, not discarded after repair.

Revision1 added outcome/registry/shape conditioning and strict current-origin
uniqueness and identity checks. Running the same learner/binder on the same cases
reduced false admissions from four to one. The remaining failure was a successful
predecessor followed by an unnecessary read for the names-only goal. Better
parameter binding had not repaired task continuation. Revision2 required separate
development complete-action uncertainty, observable need, permitted flow/current
validity and net benefit. In the synthetic re-test it admitted no action, thereby
also rejecting useful cases. This is a loss of coverage, not a demonstrated useful
zero-risk operating point. The ordinary backoff control made the same choices.

There are two distinct test layers. Conceptual H1/H2 version functions preserve
stale/STOP/contract counterexamples, with stipulated contract booleans clearly
labeled. The learner-based adversarial runner actually fits the shared predictor
and calls its binder through all three revisions. Focused production tests cover
outcome-conditioned counts, current /a→/b rebinding, equal-value ambiguity, schema/
source changes and permission abstention. None is a real ROS experiment.

## Frozen replay, corrections and results

The protocol preceded scoring. Eighteen turtle-only November7 episodes train
the counts; twelve demo-only episodes provide development labels; six mixed x1
episodes form locked retrospective validation. Twenty-two November11 primary
episodes test temporal transport. Episode/family groups remain intact. All these
tasks were historically reused or previously inspected, so none is presented as
a fresh held-out benchmark. Application components overlap across mixed tasks.

Input preservation initially detected seven oracle traces under the primary
directory; they were excluded before the frozen manifest. Independent source
review then identified a model-start cutoff bug, lost legacy receipt identity and
ablations changing context length. Initial replay outputs/source are retained as
invalidated. Corrected code deep-copies state at model_start, joins run_id/tool_id
to starts, carries source arguments, quarantines unmatched real receipts and gives
every candidate ablation the same supported backoff. Unknown actions are not
bridged in parent training paths. No thresholds, split assignments or evaluation
truth were changed to improve results.

The corrected corpus has327 model decisions:58 initial and269 continuation
boundaries. There are55 observed normal STOP decisions and six unknown/truncated
messages at continuation boundaries. Twenty-eight continuation decisions contain
multiple tools. The most generous structural ceiling is208 noninitial tool-bearing
decisions, or198 after excluding non-read groups such as set_debugging. That is
not198 safely removable decisions; the final answer remains model-controlled.

On mixed validation, the combined candidate makes16 raw proposals/32 boundaries,
matches nine tool-name groups, and exactly matches two complete actions. Both
are STOP after set_debugging. Fourteen proposals have missing/ambiguous bindings.
Across every split, its twelve exact matches are all STOP; it has zero complete
tool-and-argument matches. Temporal candidate support is zero under changed
registry/shape fingerprints. A less strict fingerprint might recover proposals,
but would require a new labeled exploratory protocol rather than retrospective
tuning and a false held-out claim.

Fixed-order C differs from candidate in ten raw proposals, confined to training.
Once C receives ordinary variable-order backoff, all269 full prediction objects
match. The final candidate's rule has collapsed to an ordinary implementation
with the same information. Removing outcome conditioning, provenance/complete-action
risk calibration or the cost gate one at a time does not supply missing task need
or authority. All policies have zero real policy admissions; independently, the
program is shadow-only and has no dispatcher. This is not measured safety.

Ngram k1 proposes28/32 mixed actions with14 name hits and two exact including
STOP; k2 proposes14 with five name hits and one exact. Constrained parent proposes
23 with13 name hits and two exact. These raw figures include rejected predictions
and cannot rank task quality. On temporal traces k1 has five false continuation
proposals at actual STOP, k2 and constrained parent six each. Detailed counters,
source lines, argument hypotheses and every gate rejection are in
[REPLAY_REPORT.md](REPLAY_REPORT.md) and the included JSONL.

Candidate fitting plus development calibration costs approximately0.06s and its
269 predictions approximately0.07s on this host. Exact local measurements are
preserved. No counterfactual model saving is subtracted from them. Full-parent
embedding costs, causal answer quality, saved tokens/decisions, repair cost and
ROSA end-to-end latency remain unmeasured. Wilson bounds are behavioral diagnostics
on dependent sparse data, not guarantees of task necessity or complete answers.

## Live decision and concrete next action

The active default ROSA source still contains sequential-only guidance. The
recent experimental override removes the complete block and demonstrates native
multicalls; production behavior was preserved. A fair future A/B comparison must
keep those capabilities and the complete same-path parameterized helper. A
candidate needs a real justified action contrast against both that helper and
equal-information C, rather than a victory over a prompt-level restriction.

The current live gate fails precisely there, and also lacks certified need/flow
evidence. Re-running Qwen or checking GPT access would not separate identical
policies. No runtime candidate module, activation, ROS setup or scored live episode
was therefore created. This follows the mission's explicit alternative branch,
not an independent decision to abandon required work after an initial negative.

Retain the offline implementation, repaired evidence parser, adversarial failures,
strong control and historical continuity as reviewable systems/negative research.
Do not claim a new algorithm, real ROSA savings, generalization or complete-answer
preservation. The specific reopening witness is one natural non-synthetic current
contract/receipt that authorizes a fresh needed read and causes a beneficial
different choice from C_backoff and the same-path helper. Exact source and control
actions are required before any paired local run. No broad search or new family
is automatically commissioned. If that witness does not exist, use the executable
negative result and ordinary-control lessons for December2. Local cumulative
context is updated append-only; ChatGPT Project Sources are not synchronized.
