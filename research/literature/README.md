# Literature survey taxonomy

This is a navigation and provenance layer over prior local reviews, not a new systematic survey. Notes explicitly identify the prior reviewer and inspected scope; this migration did not re-read every primary paper. Verify exact source version, claims and current relevance before relying on them. A paper's method or published result does not establish ROSA applicability or novelty.

## Foundational surveys and architecture

Identity and abstracts were checked at the linked original records on 2026-11-12; full papers were not reviewed for this navigation update. Consult the relevant original before relying on claims.

- [CoALA: Cognitive Architectures for Language Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02427) — architecture and action-space organization.
- [Wang et al., A Survey on Large Language Model based Autonomous Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.11432) — agent construction, applications and evaluation.
- [Qu et al., Tool Learning with Large Language Models: A Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.17935) — tool-learning stages and evaluation.
- [Chowa et al., From Language to Action](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17281) — autonomous agents and tool users.
- [Yehudai et al., A Survey on Evaluation of LLM-based Agents](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1330/) — agent evaluation.

These are broad entry points, not evidence of novelty or a substitute for mechanism-level primary literature.
